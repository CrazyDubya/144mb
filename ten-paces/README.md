# TEN PACES — 1.0 release candidate 1

Plan three seconds, then watch both sides execute simultaneously through eight
western encounters. Move, aim, fire, interact, speak, wait, dive, melee, and
reload interact with traveling bullets, ammunition, destructible cover, lamps,
smoke, injuries, morale, and four enemy temperaments.

Outlaws can surrender. Reputation, spared opponents, casualties, and allies
persist across the campaign and shape the courthouse finale.

## Controls

- Left/right: select one of the three timeline slots
- Up/down: choose any of the nine actions
- Z: commit the plan
- X: clear the plan
- C: place `SPEAK` in the selected slot
- Enter: restart with a new campaign seed
- H: hold for objective and controls; pauses play
- M: toggle audio
- Escape: quit

`./build.sh` creates `build/ten-paces.exe` and
`build/ten-paces_headless`. The harness accepts `-s`, `-N`, `-t`, and `-L`.
