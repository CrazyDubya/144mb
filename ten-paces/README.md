# TEN PACES — beta

Plan three simultaneous actions, then watch both gunfighters execute them.
Movement closes distance, aiming guarantees the next shot, and diving defeats a
shot during that beat. Enemy intentions are displayed during planning. Survive
a four-duel campaign through the saloon, train yard, canyon, and high street.

- Left/right: choose timeline slot
- Up/down: choose action
- Z: execute the three-beat plan
- X: clear the plan
- H: hold for objective and controls; pauses play
- Enter: restart
- Escape: quit

Build with `./build.sh`. The Windows game includes state-driven weapon and music
synthesis. Run `./build/ten-paces_headless -N 100` for automated campaign play.
